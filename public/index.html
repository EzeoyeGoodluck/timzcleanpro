Common Questions

How much does a clean cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the property, the type of clean (regular, deep, or specialised) and how often you book. We provide a free, no-obligation quote after a quick chat about your space — there are no hidden fees.

How quickly can you get to us?

Because we're based locally in Millom, we can usually offer availability within a few days, and often faster for short-let turnarounds or after-party cleans. Get in touch and we'll confirm the soonest slot.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. Every clean comes with our 100% satisfaction guarantee. If anything isn't up to standard, tell us and we'll put it right at no extra cost.

Can you handle emergency or same-week bookings?

In many cases, yes — particularly for after-party cleans and short-let turnarounds. Call us on 07733 811176 and we'll do our best to fit you in.

What areas do you cover?

We cover Millom, Haverigg, Silecroft, Kirksanton, Broughton-in-Furness, Bootle and the surrounding South Cumbria area. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Yes, we bring everything needed, including eco-friendly products that are safe for children, pets and tenants.
