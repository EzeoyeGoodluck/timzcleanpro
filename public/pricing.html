How We Price

What Affects the Price of Your Clean

We don't do one-size-fits-all pricing. Your quote reflects your actual space and needs, so you only pay for what's right for you. Here's what we take into account:

  • Property size (bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage)
  • Type of clean — regular, deep, or specialised
  • How often you book (regular cleans cost less per visit)
  • Condition of the property and any extras (oven, carpets, windows)
  • Location and access across South Cumbria

Why a tailored quote beats a flat rate

Flat hourly rates reward slow cleaning. We price by the job, so our team is motivated to be thorough and efficient — and you know the full cost before we start. No watching the clock, no surprise add-ons.
